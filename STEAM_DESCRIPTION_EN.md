[h1]⌚ Minidoracat MiniMap - Map Watch for B42[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

Wear a map watch to use the minimap, and install modules to unlock navigation, zombie detection and more.
Without this mod installed, the minimap stays fully available as before. Works in singleplayer and multiplayer.

[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]Minidoracat MiniMap for B42[/url]
[*] [b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI for B42[/url]
[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3801482125]Minidoracat Economy[/url] (buy or rent paid slots with in-game currency on multiplayer servers)
[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] safe to add, but watches only appear in containers and corpses not searched yet; removing it deletes watches and modules
[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report any translation issues)
[/list]

[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Loot a map watch or take one from a zombie, then wear it to use the minimap
[*] Click the "Map Watch" button in the family toolbar to open the panel, or right-click the watch and choose "Open Map Watch"
[*] Drag modules onto the panel's slots to install them and unlock more features
[*] When the battery runs out, press "Insert Battery" or "Replace Battery" in the panel
[/olist]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Wear it to use the minimap[/b]: the world map, search and your own coordinates work without one
[*] [b]Seven watches[/b]: different looks, same features, one worn at a time
[*] [b]Modules and slots[/b]: install modules to unlock navigation, resource points, zombie detection and more
[*] [b]Light Module[/b]: a light on your watch that nearby players can see too
[*] [b]Battery and charging[/b]: drains in real time, and admins can allow charging in vehicles or powered buildings
[*] [b]Status at a glance[/b]: the title bar shows the battery, and important events like a low battery pop up
[*] [b]Paid slots[/b]: buy or rent them with Economy, or use unlock cards without it
[*] [b]Admin settings[/b]: tune features, slots, batteries and how watches are obtained in one window
[/list]
