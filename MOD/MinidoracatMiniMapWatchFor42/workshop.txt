description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Loot a map watch or take one from a zombie, then wear it to use the minimap
description=[*] Click the "Map Watch" button in the family toolbar to open the panel, or right-click the watch and choose "Open Map Watch"
description=[*] Drag modules onto the panel's slots to install them and unlock more features
description=[*] When the battery runs out, press "Insert Battery" or "Replace Battery" in the panel
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Wear it to use the minimap[/b]: the world map, search and your own coordinates work without one
description=[*] [b]Seven watches[/b]: different looks, same features, one worn at a time
description=[*] [b]Modules and slots[/b]: install modules to unlock navigation, resource points, zombie detection and more
description=[*] [b]Light Module[/b]: a light on your watch that nearby players can see too
description=[*] [b]Battery and charging[/b]: drains in real time, and admins can allow charging in vehicles or powered buildings
description=[*] [b]Status at a glance[/b]: the title bar shows the battery, and important events like a low battery pop up
description=[*] [b]Paid slots[/b]: buy or rent them with Economy, or use unlock cards without it
description=[*] [b]Admin settings[/b]: tune features, slots, batteries and how watches are obtained in one window
description=[/list]
