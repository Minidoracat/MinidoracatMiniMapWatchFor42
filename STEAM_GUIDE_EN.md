=== 🚀 Quick start
[b]繁體中文版：[/b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3816991041]地圖手錶 Map Watch MOD 完整說明[/url]

The mod on Steam Workshop:
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3816406671
[olist]
[*] [b]Get a watch[/b]: loot a map watch or take one from a zombie. New watches come with a full battery.
[*] [b]Wear it[/b]: a worn, charged watch lets you use the minimap.
[*] [b]Open the panel[/b]: click [b]Map Watch[/b] in the family toolbar, or right-click the watch → "Open Map Watch".
[*] [b]Install modules[/b]: drag modules onto the panel's slots to unlock navigation, resource points and more (screwdriver, about 3 seconds).
[*] [b]Replace the battery[/b]: when it's dead, use "Insert Battery" or "Replace Battery" in the panel or the watch's right-click menu.
[/olist]
[b]The server sets the rules[/b]. Admins can change them in two places:
[list]
[*] The four sandbox pages "Minidoracat Map Watch": Features, Slots & Prices, Battery, Obtaining (the same split as the settings window).
[*] "Map Watch Admin" in Map Display Settings (the gear on the mini-map), visible to admins only.
[/list]
=== 🧰 Wear it to use the minimap
{{img:03-minimap-en.jpg}}
[list]
[*] [b]Default rule[/b]: the minimap needs a worn, charged map watch.
[*] [b]Feature modules[/b]: features like resource points and navigation also need their module.
[*] [b]Never restricted[/b]: the world map, search and your own coordinates; category search for resource points needs no module.
[*] [b]Fully open[/b]: without this mod, or with "Enable map watch" off, the minimap stays fully open and watches don't drain.
[/list]
Admins can change every one of these rules.

[h3]Installing and removing[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Three ways to install[/b]:
[list]
[*] Drag a module from your inventory onto a slot
[*] Use the module buttons in the panel
[*] Right-click the module → "Install to Map Watch"
[/list]
[*] [b]Module buttons[/b]: sorted Core, Advanced, General, with a stripe in the slot's tier color; hover one to see what it does, its range and its drain (using this server's settings).
[*] [b]Filter[/b]: slots that take several tiers get a tier filter.
[*] [b]Time[/b]: about 3 seconds, screwdriver by default; walking cancels it and the module stays where it was.
[*] [b]Keep them on you[/b]: watch and module must be on you. Modules stay on the watch and only work while it's worn.
[/list]
[h3]When a module stops[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Invalid slot[/b] (not opened, disabled, rental ended): its module stops and uses no power; remove it anytime and use another slot.
[*] [b]Slot from a removed mod[/b]:
[list]
[*] The module stops, uses no power and can be removed.
[*] The slot and unlocks are kept and return with the mod.
[*] If the module's own mod is gone too, it stays stuck on the watch until that mod returns.
[/list]
[/list]
=== 🧰 Battery and charging
[h3]Drain[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Battery[/b]: uses the vanilla Battery and drains in real time.
[*] [b]Runtime[/b]: about 72 hours on a full battery with the minimap alone.
[*] [b]Module drain[/b]: each module adds drain; the Power-Saver Core halves the total.
[*] [b]Offline and paused[/b]: no drain while offline or paused in single player by default. With "Drain while offline" on, offline time is deducted on next login (up to 30 days).
[/list]
[h3]When it's dead[/h3]
[list]
[*] All features stop by default; the minimap shows "No Signal".
[*] Admins can keep the minimap, or turn off "Needs a battery" so watches never run out.
[*] A removed battery is never fuller than when it went in: charge gained in the watch stays there, so you can't charge batteries with it.
[/list]
[h3]Charging[/h3]
Off by default (swap batteries instead). Admins can allow two ways to charge, both in real time:
[table]
[tr][th]Where[/th][th]Condition[/th][th]Time to full[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Vehicle[/td][td]Engine running, as driver or passenger[/td][td]About 6 h[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Indoors[/td][td]Grid or generator power[/td][td]About 12 h[/td][/tr]
[/table]
[list]
[*] No drain while charging.
[*] No vehicle battery or generator fuel used.
[*] No charging while offline.
[/list]

=== 🧰 Light Module
{{img:05-night-light-en.jpg}}
[list]
[*] [b]Range[/b]: lights about 4 tiles around you (adjustable).
[*] [b]Others see it[/b]: nearby players who can see you see it too, like a vanilla flashlight.
[*] [b]Drain[/b]: none while off, +100% while on (adjustable).
[*] [b]Turns off when[/b]: the battery dies, you take the watch off, the module is removed, its slot stops working, or admins disable the light.
[/list]
[h3]Turning it on and off[/h3]
[list]
[*] The panel's "Light on/off" button
[*] The watch's right-click menu
[*] [b]Hotkey[/b]: default [b]Scroll Lock[/b]; rebind under Options → Key Bindings → "Minidoracat Map Watch". No Scroll Lock on your keyboard? Use the panel or the right-click menu.
[*] [b]Vanilla "Equip/Turn On/Off Light Source" key[/b] (default [b]F[/b]): with no light in your hands, it toggles the watch light too; a flashlight in hand is toggled first, as in vanilla.
[/list]
=== 🧰 Paid slots
[list]
[*] [b]Which slots[/b]: Expansion, Advanced, Core and add-on slots.
[*] [b]Four access types[/b] (set per slot type): Free, Unlock Card, Economy, Disabled.
[*] [b]Defaults[/b]: Economy for Expansion, Advanced and Core; Free for add-on slots.
[*] [b]Tied to your account[/b]: all access (card, purchase, rental) works on any watch.
[/list]
[h3]Unlock cards[/h3]
[list]
[*] One card opens a slot for good; add-on slots use Expansion cards.
[*] A card isn't used up if the slot is already open.
[*] [b]How to use[/b]: the panel button on a locked slot, or right-click the card → "Use Unlock Card".
[/list]
[h3]Economy: buy or rent[/h3]
[list]
[*] On multiplayer servers with Minidoracat Economy, buy or rent in the panel.
[*] Bought slots can't be rented.
[*] To buy a rented slot, turn off its auto-renew first.
[*] Admins can let Economy slots accept unlock cards too (off by default).
[*] Currency, period, retry window, reminder and auto-renew are set per slot type.
[/list]
[h3]Expiry and renewal[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Expiry[/b]: a slot stops the moment its rental ends; modules stay, use no power and can be removed.
[*] [b]Payment failed[/b]: if auto-renew can't charge, it retries hourly within the retry window (24 h by default) and resumes once paid. Meanwhile you can renew but not start a new rental.
[*] [b]Reminder[/b]: 24 h before the end by default.
[*] [b]Price changes[/b]: if rent, currency or period changes, players with auto-renew must press "Accept New Terms"; you're only charged what you agreed to.
[/list]
[h3]When Economy isn't available[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]No Economy, an outdated one, or single player[/b]: Economy slots use unlock cards instead; the panel explains.
[*] [b]Split-screen players 2–4[/b] (multiplayer): no paid access and no unlock cards.
[/list]
=== ⚙️ Player settings
[list]
[*] [b]Where[/b]: Map Display Settings (the gear on the mini-map, or the paw-print button on the world map) → "Add-ons" group → "Map Watch". The same page is in the game's Options → Mods.
[*] [b]Who sees it[/b]: every player.
[*] [b]Saved[/b]: on your own computer, not in the save.
[/list]
[h3]Options[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Map watch sound volume[/b]: 0–100% (default 70%), on top of the game's master volume; 0% plays nothing.
[list]
[*] You can also set it right above the battery bar in the watch panel.
[*] When you adjust it in the panel or this category, your watch's install sound plays once at the new volume after you stop, so you can compare (the Options → Mods page doesn't preview).
[/list]
[*] [b]Ping on scheduled scan updates[/b] (off by default): a short ping when zombie dots or vehicles and animals are rescanned; once if both update together.
[list]
[*] [b]Only plays when the server sets the minimap scan interval to scheduled[/b]; live updates (the default) don't ping.
[*] Your watch must be able to use that feature.
[/list]
[*] [b]Show Map Watch button[/b] (on by default): the Map Watch button in the family toolbar.
[list]
[*] Untick it to hide it right away (in Options → Mods, press Apply).
[*] You can still open the panel by right-clicking the map watch in your inventory and choosing "Open Map Watch".
[/list]
[/list]
=== ⚙️ Admin settings window
{{img:08-admin-en.jpg}}
[list]
[*] [b]Where[/b]: Map Display Settings → "Admin" group → "Map Watch Admin", then press "Open Map Watch Settings".
[*] [b]Who sees it[/b]: admins and moderators (not gm or players); always in single player.
[*] [b]Six pages[/b]:
[list]
[*] Overview: presets Relaxed / Standard (recommended) / Hardcore, and current settings
[*] Features, Slots & Prices, Battery, Obtaining, Zombie Drops
[/list]
[/list]
[h3]Applying changes[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Review & Apply[/b] lists every change first (old → new) and warns about rent, currency or period changes.
[*] [b]A reason is required[/b]: the reason and changes go to admin-audit.log next to the server settings file, plus the server log.
[*] The server re-checks permission.
[*] [b]Two admins at once[/b]: the later one is blocked and reloads the current settings, so the first change isn't overwritten. Editing the file directly also counts as a change.
[/list]
[h3]Where settings live[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Values[/b]: in the sandbox; saved and pushed by the server in multiplayer, in the save in single player.
[*] [b]Lists[/b]: drop rules, add-on module drain and per-slot add-on settings live in the server settings file.
[/list]
=== ⚙️ Server settings file
[list]
[*] [b]Location[/b]: Zomboid/Lua/MinidoracatWatch/server name/server-settings.json (sp_save name in single player)
[*] [b]Missing file[/b]: a default with 10 drop rules is written.
[*] [b]Applies[/b]: within 10 seconds, no restart.
[*] [b]Invalid file[/b]: if the JSON or any rule is invalid, the whole rule list is rejected and the last valid one kept; the server log names the rule and the problem.
[/list]

[h3]moduleDrains and addonSlots[/h3]
[list]

[*] Settings survive a mod being temporarily removed.
[/list]

=== ⚠️ Known limitations
[list]
[*] [b]Required versions[/b]:
[list]
[*] Minidoracat MiniMap 42.21.0-0.37.0 or later: with an older one the watch has no effect, the minimap stays open, and you're told to update.
[*] Minidoracat UI 42.21.0-0.8.0 or later: needed for the panel and settings window.
[*] Minidoracat Economy 42.21.0-0.8.0 or later: buying and renting slots, on a multiplayer server.
[*] Economy 42.21.0-0.11.0 or later: "Sell in Economy Center" and freezing rentals while an add-on mod is removed. Older versions only stop auto-renew charges while that mod is gone; the rental still ends on time.
[/list]
[*] [b]Economy removed and re-added[/b]: rentals aren't compensated (they keep counting).
[*] [b]No-steam servers[/b]: no identity check, so a player renamed to an offline player may use that player's opened slots.
[*] [b]Changed drop or loot settings[/b]: already-searched corpses and already-spawned container loot aren't affected.
[/list]
=== ❓ FAQ
[h3]The minimap works without a watch?[/h3]
[list]
[*] The admin may have turned off "Enable map watch" or set the minimap rule to "No watch needed".
[*] An outdated MiniMap also leaves it open, with a notice.
[/list]
[h3]The minimap says "No Signal"?[/h3]
[list]
[*] The watch is dead or has no battery.
[*] Insert or replace one.
[/list]
[h3]A module shows as disabled?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Its slot isn't open, the rental ended, or the watch is dead.
[*] It also won't work if the admin turned its feature off.
[*] Move it to a working slot.
[/list]
[h3]Detector or Scanner installed, but nothing shows on the minimap?[/h3]
[list]
[*] The server's minimap settings may have zombie positions and heat, or vehicle and animal icons, turned off; the modules can't show them then.
[*] Ask the admin to turn them on in the minimap settings.
[/list]
[h3]Can't install a module?[/h3]
Check that:
[list]
[*] you have a screwdriver (servers can drop this)
[*] the watch and module are on you
[*] the slot is open and accepts that class (Core modules only fit the Core Slot)
[*] you don't walk while installing
[/list]
[h3]Battery dropped a lot after logging back in?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Offline drain is off by default; only with "Drain while offline" is it deducted, up to 30 days.
[*] Single-player pause doesn't drain by default either.
[/list]
[h3]Do paid slots carry over to another watch?[/h3]
[list]
[*] Yes, they're tied to your account.
[*] The modules stay on the old watch, so move them over.
[/list]
[h3]Paid slots in single player?[/h3]
[list]
[*] There's no Economy payment; Economy slots use unlock cards.
[*] Or set them to Free in the settings window.
[/list]
[h3]Can I charge batteries with the watch?[/h3]
[list]
[*] No. A removed battery is never fuller than when it went in.
[/list]
[h3]Putting on the watch takes off the arm guard on that arm?[/h3]
[list]
[*] In vanilla, the wrist and forearm armor on the same arm can't be worn together, and vanilla watches work the same way.
[*] If the server runs Pack Mule with its "Slot: Watch" option on, map watches move to Pack Mule's watch slot just like vanilla watches and can be worn with arm guards.
[*] Take off a watch you were already wearing and put it back on once.
[/list]
